CAMPAIGN / LEAD INTELLIGENCE

LeadFlow

LeadFlow is a campaign-focused lead tracking web app that connects incoming leads to their source—such as Google Ads, social campaigns, website forms, or referrals—so teams can see which channels are generating the strongest opportunities.

It helps track qualified leads through follow-up and conversion, making it easier to understand which campaigns and strategies are actually driving results.
